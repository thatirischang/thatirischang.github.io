* The film *John Rabe* is a historical drama drawn from real events. It tells the story of the German businessman John Rabe who, during the Nanking Massacre of 1937, used his standing and his influence to establish a safety zone, and so saved tens of thousands of Chinese civilians. With a careful narrative and a fine craft, the film shows the courage and the human conscience of Rabe in conditions of the most extreme distress, and casts into sharp relief the light of human nature within war. The film has been widely praised across the world and has received a number of international film awards; it stands as a much-honored work of historical cinema.
