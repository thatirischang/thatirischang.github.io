* *City of Life and Death* (Chinese: 《南京！南京！》, *Nanjing! Nanjing!*) is a film, set on the subject of the Nanking Massacre, by the mainland Chinese director Lu Chuan. Filming began on October 27, 2007; the film was released in mainland China on April 22, 2009. That same year, *City of Life and Death* won the Golden Shell at the San Sebastián International Film Festival, in Spain.
